# Coworkerz · Custom executive roles and a live roster · v1

**Deliverable:** Implement this feature in the existing Next.js App Router / Drizzle SQLite app. Return **Changes**, **Evidence** (pass, fail, or not run with reason), and **Remaining risk**. Read `AGENTS.md` first. This extends **Customize bots**; changing a role is no longer just changing a badge or job description.

**Pass when:** During an open session, the CEO can add a fourth executive with a role not shipped in source code, address it through the UI and API, and get a reply from that executive. Replacing the CFO must leave the original CFO’s conversations and memories intact, not give them to the replacement. Both results must survive reload and server restart.

## Confirmed product decisions

- **Add, remove, or replace executives.** Offer suggested role names **and** free-text entry. A suggestion is not an allowlist. Each executive is independently addressable; a custom role must not be an alias for CTO, CFO, or COO.
- **Changing the executive’s role means replacement.** Explain before saving: the old executive leaves the active roster, its history remains available to the CEO, and a new executive starts with a separate identity and no personal history. In contrast, editing a bot’s personal display name, job, or skill prompt still edits that same bot.
- **Use the live roster on every turn**, including ongoing sessions. A direct question goes to the selected active executive. For a whole-team question, select the responder by relevance to the active bots’ roles, jobs, and skills—not by a fixed three-role cycle.
- **Stage only the current speaker and listener in 3D.** All active executives remain selectable in the roster, including those offscreen. The CEO remains the human participant.
- **The CEO reviews and selects a permission preset.** Role titles are not grants. Neither a suggested title nor replacement of an executive conveys that executive’s access or memories.

**Ask rather than invent product policy.** Before implementing a choice that depends on it, confirm the suggestion list and actual preset grants, any active-roster limit or duplicate-title policy, and what happens on a relevance tie, an empty roster, or removal during an in-flight turn. If shared transcript visibility before a bot joined is needed, clarify it separately from ownership of personal memories. Present specific options; do not reopen the decisions above or silently ship guessed defaults.

## Implementation constraints

1. **Identity and migration.** Trace `src/lib/executives.ts`, `src/lib/executive-profile-store.ts` and `src/db/schema.ts`. Use a persistent identity independent of the human-readable role, display name, and permission preset. An active-list removal must not cascade-delete history. Make replacement atomic. Generate a forward Drizzle migration; preserve existing sessions, role attribution, profile overrides, model assignments, and ACLs without a reseed. Do not edit applied migrations. Document how existing role-coded API clients transition; an old CFO address must never silently target its successor.
2. **One roster, from settings to the next turn.** Update `src/components/CustomizeBotsDialog.tsx`, the profile APIs, boardroom composer and model settings. Keep keyboard access, save/cancel, inline errors and focus return; distinguish **Edit profile** from **Replace executive**. Trace `/api/sessions`, `/api/ceo-message`, `/api/boardroom` and `/api/deliberate/step`. Resolve targets against persisted active identities on the server; a stale preview is not authority to speak. Handle a missing peer without inventing a CFO or COO. In `src/lib/opencode.ts`, audit `pickRelevantExecutive`, `planDeliberationStep`, model selection and local fallback. Use only the speaker’s skill prompt; preserve the six-key turn JSON contract. Do not classify every unfamiliar executive as operations or use the COO’s model by default.
3. **Memory and permissions.** Audit role-indexed observers, persona summaries and journal paths in `src/lib/memory.ts`; do not backfill old private memories onto new members. Use stable IDs for storage and filenames, not user-entered titles. In `src/lib/enterprise-knowledge.ts` and the versioned APIs, enforce the selected preset server-side for retrieval and graph access. Unknown/retired identities must not fall back to CEO privileges. Keep `CEO`, `ALL`, `JOINT` and `EXTERNAL_AGENT` distinct from custom identities. Keep governed knowledge separate from personal memory.
4. **Room representation.** In `src/components/VirtualOfficeCanvas.tsx`, decouple onstage avatars, nameplates, camera focus and picking from the three original role keys. The avatar playing the speaker is not the speaker’s permanent identity. Reuse the room and avatars without rebuilding the renderer on roster edits. Do not allocate a mesh for every offscreen bot. Before producing new art, agree per-asset triangle/texture/draw-call/particle budgets, hero LOD0–LOD3 and mobile overdraw limits per `docs/art-budgets.md`.
